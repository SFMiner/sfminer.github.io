**Matter and Energy in Change: Study Guide**  
This study guide covers the big ideas about how matter changes, how we identify substances, and how energy moves during chemical reactions.  
**Big Idea 1: Substances and Properties**  
A **substance** is a specific type of matter. Every substance has **properties** that help us tell them apart. A property is something you can observe or measure.

| Property Type | What to Look For | Lab Illustration |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **Color** | What color is it? | Sugar, salt, and baking soda are all white. |
| **Texture** | How does it feel? | Is it grainy, like salt, or fine, like powder? |
| **Smell** | Does it have a scent? | Smell is a very specific property for identification. |
| **State** | Is it solid, liquid, or gas? | Ice, water, and steam are the same substance in different states. |
| **Reaction** | How does it act with others? | Does it bubble when mixed with vinegar? |

**Big Idea 2: Physical vs. Chemical Changes**  
The main difference between these changes is whether the substance itself changes or just its look/speed.  
**Physical Change**  
The substance stays the same. No new molecules are made.

**Chemical Change**  
Atoms rearrange to form brand-new substances.

* **Example:** "Elephant's Toothpaste" (ET) or mixing two clear liquids to get a cloudy solid (**precipitate**).  
* **Evidence of a Reaction:**  
  1. **Gas/Bubbles** are produced.  
  2. **Color change** occurs.  
  3. **Precipitate** (a new solid forms in a liquid).  
  4. **Temperature change** (gets hotter or colder).  
  5. **New smell** is created.

\--------------------------------------------------------------------------------  
**Big Idea 3: Reactants, Products, and Mass**  
In a chemical reaction, atoms do not appear or disappear; they just move around into new spots.

* **Reactants:** The starting substances you put in.  
* **Products:** The new substances you get at the end.  
* **Conservation of Mass:** The total mass of the reactants always equals the total mass of the products.  
* **Illustration:** If you mix vinegar and baking soda in a sealed bag, the bag stays the same weight (mass) because the gas atoms are trapped inside.

\--------------------------------------------------------------------------------  
**Big Idea 4: Energy (Heat)**  
Chemical reactions either release energy or absorb it. We can feel this as a change in temperature.

* **Exothermic:** Releases heat. The surroundings get **warmer**.  
  * *Examples:* Hand warmers, burning wood, rusting iron.  
* **Endothermic:** Absorbs heat. The surroundings get **cooler**.  
  * *Examples:* Instant cold packs, photosynthesis, baking soda mixed with vinegar.

**Glossary of Key Terms**

* **Chemical Reaction:** A process where atoms rearrange to form new substances.  
* **Combustion:** The process of burning a fuel with oxygen.  
* **Endothermic:** A reaction that absorbs heat, making things feel colder.  
* **Exothermic:** A reaction that releases heat, making things feel warmer.  
* **Fuel:** A substance that releases energy when it burns.  
* **Precipitate:** A new solid that forms when two liquids are mixed during a reaction.  
* **Product:** The ending substance in a chemical reaction.  
* **Property:** A characteristic used to identify a substance (like color or smell).  
* **Reactant:** The starting substance in a chemical reaction.  
* **Substance:** A type of matter with specific properties.

**Unit Quiz**  
**1\. Which of these is a physical change?**

* (a) Wood burning  
* (b) Ice melting  
* (c) Iron rusting

**2\. In a chemical reaction, what happens to the atoms?**

* (a) Some atoms are destroyed.  
* (b) New atoms are created.  
* (c) Atoms rearrange but none disappear.

**3\. If a reaction makes a cup feel cold, it is:**

* (a) Exothermic  
* (b) Endothermic  
* (c) Combustion

**4\. What are the two reactants needed for combustion (burning)?**

* (a) Fuel and Oxygen  
* (b) *CO*2 and Water Vapor  
* (c) Iron and Vinegar

**5\. True or False: If you burn a piece of paper, the mass disappears and no longer exists.**

* (a) True  
* (b) False

**6\. Which property is very specific and helpful for identifying a substance at a crime scene?**

* (a) Color  
* (b) Texture  
* (c) Smell

\--------------------------------------------------------------------------------  
**Answer Key**

1. **(b) Ice melting** (The substance stays the same).  
2. **(c) Atoms rearrange but none disappear** (Law of Conservation of Mass).  
3. **(b) Endothermic** (It absorbs heat from the cup).  
4. **(a) Fuel and Oxygen** (These are the starting materials).  
5. **(b) False** (The atoms turned into invisible gases like *CO*2 and water vapor).  
6. **(c) Smell** (It is very specific to each substance).
