Big Idea 1

Substances & Properties

A substance is a type of matter.
Every substance has properties — things you can observe or measure — that help us tell them apart.

PropertyWhat to Look ForExample
ColorWhat color is it?Sugar, salt & baking soda are all white
TextureHow does it feel?Grainy (salt) vs. fine powder
SmellDoes it have a scent?Very specific — great for ID
StateSolid, liquid, or gas?Ice, water, steam = same substance
ReactionHow does it act with others?Does it bubble with vinegar?
Big Idea 2

Physical vs. Chemical Changes

⚡ Physical Change
The substance stays the same.
No new molecules are made.

Example: Ice melting
H₂O solid → H₂O liquid

Only the speed of molecules changes.
🔥 Chemical Change
Atoms rearrange to make brand-new substances.

Examples:
• Elephant's Toothpaste
• Two liquids → cloudy solid (precipitate)

You cannot easily undo it.
Big Idea 2 — continued

5 Signs of a Chemical Change

  • 💨 Gas / Bubbles are produced
  • 🎨 Color change occurs
  • 🧊 Precipitate — a new solid forms in a liquid
  • 🌡️ Temperature change — gets hotter or colder
  • 👃 New smell is created
Big Idea 3

Reactants, Products & Mass

In Every Reaction…
Reactants = starting substances you put in

Products = new substances you get out

Atoms don't appear or disappear — they just move to new spots.
⚖️ Conservation of Mass
The total mass of reactants always equals the total mass of products.

Example: Vinegar + baking soda in a sealed bag → the bag weighs the same before and after. Gas atoms are still inside!
Big Idea 4

Energy in Reactions (Heat)

🔥 Exothermic
Releases heat.
Surroundings get WARMER.

Examples:
• Hand warmers
• Burning wood
• Rusting iron
❄️ Endothermic
Absorbs heat.
Surroundings get COOLER.

Examples:
• Instant cold packs
• Photosynthesis
• Baking soda + vinegar

Memory tip: EXOthermic = EXits heat outward 🔥  |  ENDOthermic = ENters heat inward ❄️

Big Idea 5

Combustion & Climate

Combustion is a chemical reaction where a fuel burns.

The Reaction
Fuel + Oxygen
↓
CO₂ + Water Vapor + Energy
🌍 The Climate Link
Burning fossil fuels releases CO₂.

CO₂ is a greenhouse gas — it traps heat in the atmosphere → climate change.
Key Terms

Glossary

  • Chemical Reaction — Atoms rearrange to form new substances
  • Combustion — Burning a fuel with oxygen
  • Endothermic — Absorbs heat; things feel colder
  • Exothermic — Releases heat; things feel warmer
  • Fuel — Substance that releases energy when it burns
  • Precipitate — New solid that forms when two liquids mix
  • Product — Ending substance in a reaction
  • Property — Characteristic used to identify a substance
  • Reactant — Starting substance in a reaction
  • Substance — A type of matter with specific properties
